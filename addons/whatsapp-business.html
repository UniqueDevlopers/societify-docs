WhatsApp Business API

Version 1.0.0 · Requires Societify core 1.8.0 or later

Official WhatsApp Business messaging for every society: bills and receipts with the PDF attached, Meta template sync and creation, per-event routing to owners, tenants and family, opt-in records, delivery and read receipts, and a two-way inbox. Meta Cloud API and Twilio, plus self-hosted Evolution API and WAHA for those who accept the risk.
