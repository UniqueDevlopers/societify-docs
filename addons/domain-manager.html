Domain & Subdomain Management

Version 1.0.0 · Requires Societify core 1.8.0 or later

Give every society its own web address. Automatic subdomains under your platform domain on any hosting, plus verified custom domains that are re-checked after they go live. Residents and administrators reach their society on a branded URL; payments, mobile and licensing stay on your platform host. HTTPS is arranged by your hosting; this addon reports what it observes and never issues or renews certificates.
