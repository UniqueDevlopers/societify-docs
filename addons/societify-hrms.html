Societify HRMS

Version 1.0.0 · Requires Societify core 1.8.0 or later · Mobile build 1.0.0

Societify HRMS – Global Human Resources & Workforce Management Suite: departments, designations and an employee directory, domestic worker registry with verification, shifts, attendance and timesheets, leave with balances and holidays, payroll with payslips and per-country statutory rules, role-based access, and mobile self-service (check-in, leave, payslips) for guards, staff, collectors, in-house vendors and office roles.
