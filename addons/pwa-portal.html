Resident Web Portal

Version 1.0.0 · Requires Societify core 1.8.0 or later

A browser-based home for your residents. Bills, payments, visitors, maintenance requests, notices and bookings in a fast, responsive web app that installs to the home screen on phones and tablets and works just as well on a desktop — no app store, no download, nothing to keep updated.
